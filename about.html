About Skillbound

We built the school we wished existed when we switched careers.

Skillbound started in 2019 as a weekend workshop for four people learning to code in a co-working space. It's now a four-module cohort model used by 4,000+ graduates — but the method hasn't changed: build something real, get it torn apart by someone who does the job, fix it.

How it runs

Eight weeks, four modules, one deliverable each.

The order is fixed on purpose — you can't critique work that doesn't exist yet, and you can't ship something that hasn't been critiqued.

MOD-01 · WEEKS 1–2
Foundations

Tooling, fundamentals, and your first small brief — due end of week 2, no exceptions.

MOD-02 · WEEKS 3–5
Build

The main project. Weekly check-ins with your mentor; scope is locked by week 4.

MOD-03 · WEEK 6
Critique

A live session where your cohort and mentor rework your project's weakest parts together.

MOD-04 · WEEKS 7–8
Ship

Polish, deploy, and present to a panel of practitioners currently hiring for the role.
